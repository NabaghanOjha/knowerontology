The central clarification

Two claims, deliberately kept apart.

A reader may accept KCM’s inferential constraint without accepting its positive ontology. This separation is central to Version 2.0.

Thesis AInferential constraint

Structure alone does not settle presence.

No description of organisation, accessibility or dynamics, considered by itself and without an added bridge or identity principle, entails or decisively establishes that a subject is present.

The constraint is symmetric: such a description does not establish absence either.

Adoes not entailB
Thesis BOntological postulate

The Knower is irreducible.

KCM postulates the Knower as the condition under which appearance occurs, rather than as something generated by the structures appearing within experience.

This is KCM’s distinctive proposal, and it carries an independent burden of argument.

From evidence to attribution

Evidence does not interpret itself.

Neural, behavioural and computational findings can legitimately change our confidence about consciousness. But the route from structure to subjective presence requires a bridge principle: an explicit and defeasible assumption connecting the evidence to the conclusion.

Every attribution of consciousness is evidence plus a principle connecting that evidence to presence.

An explicitly revisable proposal

What KCM does not yet solve.

KCM’s present contribution is primarily conceptual and methodological. Its empirical programme remains in development.

01

Comparing bridge principles

What standards should determine when one structure-to-subject bridge is better justified than another?

02

Individuating knowers

What grounds the claim that there is one Knower, many knowers, or a change in the boundaries of a subject?

03

Machine consciousness

What evidence could rationally change credence without confusing sophisticated output with subjective presence?

04

Empirical development

Which proposed contrasts can become discriminating studies rather than reinterpretations of findings compatible with several theories?

Discussion and criticism invited

KCM is offered as a research programme, not an established theory.

The most useful engagement will identify hidden bridge principles, challenge the separation of presence from structure, or propose empirical cases that sharpen the framework.
