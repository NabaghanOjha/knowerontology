KCM | Proposed Empirical Research

An Exploratory Neurophenomenological Test of Presence–Complexity Dissociation in Minimal-Content States

Investigating the relationship between reported awareness, experiential content and neural signal complexity.

Research status: Proposal for scientific collaboration and methodological review. Not yet conducted or preregistered.

Read Full Research Protocol (PDF) ↗

01 | The research question

Can awareness remain present when experiential content is minimal?

Some contemplative reports describe states in which thoughts, imagery and ordinary mental activity become markedly reduced, while awareness is nevertheless reported as present.

The proposed study asks whether such reports can be examined alongside changes in neural signal complexity, without assuming that either measure independently establishes the presence or absence of consciousness.

02 | The theoretical motivation

Why KCM motivates this investigation

The Knower Centred Model distinguishes three aspects of experience: the Knower (K), Knowing (N) and the Known (C).

Within this framework, changes in the structure or richness of experiential content do not, by themselves, settle whether subjective presence persists.

The study therefore investigates an empirical contrast motivated by KCM. It does not treat the model's ontological postulates as experimentally established.

03 | Proposed methodology

Bringing first-person experience and neuroscience into dialogue

The protocol proposes an exploratory neurophenomenological approach combining EEG recordings with structured first-person reports.

01

EEG recording

Measure neural activity during the states and comparisons specified in the full protocol.

02

Signal complexity

Examine EEG Lempel–Ziv complexity as a measure of signal diversity, without equating it directly with consciousness.

03

First-person reports

Collect structured phenomenological descriptions to distinguish reported awareness from the richness and accessibility of experiential content.

04 | Scientific significance

What would the investigation contribute?

The study aims to examine how neural complexity relates to reports of minimal experiential content and continuing awareness.

Different patterns of results could support, constrain or challenge specific empirical bridge hypotheses. Alternative explanations, including limitations of retrospective reporting and EEG measures, require careful consideration.

No single outcome would establish the existence of an irreducible Knower or conclusively settle competing theories of consciousness.

05 | Research transparency

A proposal open to scientific examination

This protocol is publicly available for methodological criticism, refinement and potential collaboration.

Current status: The study has not yet been conducted or preregistered. No experimental results are being claimed.

The full document provides the proposed methodological and analytical details and should be consulted before interpreting the research aims.
